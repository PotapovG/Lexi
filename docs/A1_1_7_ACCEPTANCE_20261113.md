### Six-language integrity and learner safety

- Six locale keys **ru/en/zh/ar/es/pt** are present for newly authored
  grammar explanations and material in the corresponding authorial sources.
  Existing validation and scoring tests cover these values in each language.
- Correct alternatives and full-sentence meanings are shown *after* scoring;
  new exercises do not use English as a placeholder translation for another
  locale.
- A **human, independent native-language Greek + six-locale editorial pass is
  not evidenced by CI**. This remains open. The older Greek Life single-scene
  prompts still primarily provide Russian/English text; the #17 enhancement
  specifically targets the existing Quick practice rather than claiming all
  Greek Life screens are translated into six languages.
- Authored new phonetic tasks never invent Pocket WAV URLs; in CI the word
  lookups have no matching files for the new exercises, so textual fallback
  is exercised. **An actual audio discrimination test cannot be considered
  completed without approved, pre-rendered samples.**

### Device and timing

- Existing Quick accepts timed sessions (10–60 minute settings); the
  scenario bundler uses at most five of the normal 60 available question slots.
  Partial scenarios are deliberately removed from normal scope/exclusion
  paths, including requests for fewer than five questions. Individual failed
  cards remain reachable via existing weak/SRS.
- The existing question/finish panel has responsive CSS for narrow layouts,
  including a 320px breakpoint and vertical source text. **This is a static
  contract plus automated grading tests, not evidence of a real-device
  visual/accessibility sign-off.** Mobile touch + offline Pocket audio QA remain.
